## Executive Summary

The inmate lookup niche has genuine traffic potential but carries **significant risks**. The national head keywords are dominated by government (.gov) sites which are nearly impossible to outrank. The real opportunity is in **hyper-local, state/county-level guide pages** that explain HOW to search for inmates — not sites that actually host inmate data. Verdict: **CONDITIONAL GO** with strategy pivot.

---

## Red Flags

1. **YMYL Classification** — Google requires high trust for legal content. Fix: Be a directory, not a data provider.
2. **Data accuracy** — Hosting real data = liability + maintenance. Fix: Link to official sources only.
3. **National terms = government wins** — Fix: Only target hyper-local long-tail keywords.
